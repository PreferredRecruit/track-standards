Privacy

Updated October 19, 2026. Track Standards (trackandfieldstandards.com) is run by Preferred Recruit.

What we collect when you use the site

  • What you search, without your name. When you search the standards, pick an event, a grade or a state, open a school or an athlete's results, or see one of our evaluation offers, we save that action with a random ID stored in your browser. We use it to improve the site, to decide which guides and pages to build next, and to show offers that match the event you're looking at.
  • Your recent searches, in your own browser. Your last event, mark and grade are kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Page analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies.

Free accounts

You can use the site without an account. A free account lets you see full college lists, full results lists and unlimited Recruiting AI answers. If you create one, we store:

  • Your email and password. Passwords are stored encrypted by our sign-in provider (Supabase). We never see them.
  • Your profile. Whether you're an athlete, parent, coach or fan, plus team, grade, state, a GPA range and your best marks.
  • Contact details you choose to add. An athlete can add a parent's email. A parent can add a phone number and, only if they tick the box, agree to texts from Preferred Recruit about their athlete's recruiting. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.
  • Your searches on the site. Your search activity is linked to your account so the site can show guides and offers that match your events and level.

We use your profile to decide which guides and offers to show you on this site. If you ask for the free recruitment evaluation, Preferred Recruit may contact you about it. For athletes under 18, recruiting contact goes to a parent, never to the athlete directly. You must be 13 or older to create an account. We don't sell data, and we don't share it with advertising platforms.

What we never collect

We don't store IP addresses with search data, and we don't save what you type into the Recruiting AI chat. Without an account, we don't ask for names, emails or phone numbers.

The free recruitment evaluation

The evaluation runs on Preferred Recruit's own site. When you click through, the link carries the event, grade and mark you searched so the evaluation can start where you left off. Anything you enter there is covered by Preferred Recruit's privacy terms on that site.

College coaches

Coaches who sign in to publish their standards give us their work email so we can verify they're on staff. While a coach is signed in, the site records which schools and published results they view, linked to their program, so we know how coaches use the site. Coach emails are never shown on the site.

Results data

College results come from public performance lists (TFRRS) and official school sites.

Questions, changes or deleting your account

You can edit your profile any time from "My account". To delete your account and everything linked to it, or to ask a question, contact Preferred Recruit through thepreferredrecruit.com.
