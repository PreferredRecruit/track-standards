# trackandfieldstandards.com: UI, UX and functionality audit (2026-11-03)

Ryan: "how can we beef up the standards website ui // ux and functionality".

How it was done: 15 independent reviewers (one lens each: phone landing, search, accounts, rankings, results, Recruiting AI, speed,
accessibility, design-system conformance, copy, static pages, feature gaps, data trust, tracking, quality score) worked from the
same evidence: 164 screenshots of the live site at 375 px, 1280 px and phone dark mode (`design-shots/audit/`), per-page load
metrics (`metrics.json`), 14 days of the site's own analytics (`DATA.md`), and the code. They returned 176 findings. The merge,
verification and synthesis agents were cut off by the account's weekly usage limit (resets Nov 6), so this report was assembled by
hand: the 31 critical and high findings were re-checked in the code or on the live site ("checked" below); the medium and low ones
are listed as the reviewers reported them. Evidence files are in `design-shots/audit/`.

Decisions the audit respected (not up for reversal here): free to use, no paywall, SEO pages never gated; the 15-college wall and
"finish your profile first" rule; pop-ups only, one prompt at a time, never on arrival; Recruiting AI stays a data engine (no LLM);
Precision Futurism (tokens, one accent, Inter, no runtime UI libraries); official claims only; the new "show the what, sell the how"
rule (no DIY outreach kits on the site).

## Where the product stands

The data and the breadth are already ahead of the competition; the product around them is not yet at the bar. Light mode is
disciplined, motion and feedback are at the Linear level, search is instant once the app boots, nothing scrolls sideways, every
field is 16 px, every image has alt text. What drags it down is a handful of mechanical defects that hit the biggest cohorts:
phones could not finish onboarding at all (fixed today), the mark field accepts impossible marks and reports them as Target level,
one in nine searches ends in a wrong sentence, 43% of school pages show a column of dashes, dark mode blanks out four controls, the
sheet that is the site's lead engine stacks on top of the account wall and breaks its own design spec, and a cold phone load shows
three different pages before the real one. Scorecard: 16 of 28.

## Do first

Ordered by visitors affected × severity ÷ effort. S = under 2 hours, M = a day, L = several days.

1. **Phones could not finish onboarding or the readiness check: the Continue, Back and Skip buttons were hidden.** `standards.css:1195`
   hid `.onbnav` under 760 px for the profile page's site switcher, but the onboarding wizard footer (`onboard.js:162`) and the
   readiness footer (`readiness.js:50`) use the same class. 306 phone sign-ups in 14 days, 25 finished (8%); desktop 264 of 339 (78%).
   **Fixed today**: the switcher is now `.onbsite`. Measure: mobile `finished ÷ signup` in `insights.site_events` (target: desktop's 78%).
   Still open from the same lens (S): drop the Confirm-password field, `enterkeyhint="go"`, `interactive-widget=resizes-content` in
   the viewport meta so the sheet rises above the keyboard; on phones say "Open your Mail app" instead of Gmail/Outlook web buttons.
2. **The mark field accepts impossible marks and reports them as Target.** (checked) `parseMark` (`app.js:106-129`) has a plausible
   range per event but returns the raw number when nothing fits: "5.3" in the 1600m fits 1,086 colleges at Target; "49.8" in the
   800m is "Target level at Oregon" and is then saved as the visitor's mark on every school page. iPhone keypads have no colon, so
   "421" and "4.2150" are the normal phone input. Fix (S): return null below the floor; read 3 to 4 digit integers as m:ss and
   "4.21.50" as m:ss.ss for events of 800m and up; specific hint "That doesn't look like a 1600m time. Type it like 4:21.5 (4.21.5
   works on a phone keypad)"; placeholder "e.g. 4:21.5 or 4.21.5". Files: `app.js` parseMark, submit (~2457), `schoolResult` (~606),
   `onboard.js` mark field. Measure: zero-fit searches (11%) and "my mark looks off" feedback should both drop.
3. **Zero-fit searches end in "No colleges match these filters." with no filters set.** (checked) `app.js:495` uses one empty string
   for "no fits" and "filtered to nothing". 824 sessions in 14 days. Fix (S): when `all.length === 0` and no filter is set, headline
   "12.90 in the men's 100m is 0.09 s from the chart", body "The nearest listed level is Walk-On 12.81 (NCAA D3, CUNYAC). Many D3 and
   NAIA teams take walk-ons below their listed marks: ask the coach.", plus "See what each level takes ›" to `/how-fast/<event>.html`
   and the readiness link. Hide the "0 Target · 0 Recruit · 0 Walk-On" row. Never-hater framing. Measure: clicks on either link.
4. **43% of school pages show a Recruit column of dashes, and the Walk-On header wraps to "WALK-O / N".** (checked) D3 and NAIA
   rules are `[8, null, 40]` (no Recruit level because D3 gives no athletic scholarships), but `school()` (`app.js:650-665`) and
   `scripts/seo/schools.mjs` always print three columns. Fix (S): two columns (Target, Walk-On) when the division has no Recruit
   level, with the explanation in a one-line caption under every table ("Set from the 2026 WIAC list · Target = 8th best · Walk-On =
   40th · D3 gives no athletic scholarships, so there is no Recruit level · How these are set ›"), generated from the rules so it
   can't drift. Same caption fixes the finding that the numbers have no visible source until a collapsed fold five screens down.
   Also `white-space:nowrap` on the last header and letter-spacing 0 on phone headers.
5. **Dark mode blanks out the site's controls.** (checked) `ui.css:22` remaps `--pr-black` to near-white in dark mode, but
   `.chk button{color:#fff}` (`ui.css:67`) and four rules in `standards.css` (514, 528, 731, 1359) keep white text: "Search colleges"
   on about 7,300 static pages, Filters/Done on Results and Rankings, the "See who'd recruit you" pill. The sign-in sheet stays
   white (`standards.css:691`) while its labels use the dark ink token: "Email" and "Password" at 1.09:1, black input boxes on a
   white card. Fix (S): `color:var(--pr-card)` in those rules, `.eval.acc{background:var(--pr-card);color:var(--pr-ink)}` and the
   sheet's hard-coded greys to tokens; add a `design:check` rule that flags `var(--pr-black)` next to `#fff`. Add a `dark` flag
   to the page-view event so the share of dark-mode visitors is known.
6. **The evaluation sheet stacks on the account wall and breaks its own spec.** (checked, `flow-popup-after-search.png`) The wall
   ("See all 163 colleges · Create free account") and the pop-up ("163 programs fit your 52.30 400m") appear together, two CTAs
   for two different things; the sheet's header is a violet gradient with a 3D striped floor and five tilted logo cards, against
   DESIGN.md and POPUP-SYSTEM.md (white sheet, no logo). Fix (S for the trigger, M for the sheet): fire `results-end` only once
   the wall has scrolled out of view or a filter changed (`armOffer`, `app.js:289`); rebuild the sheet header to the spec (count
   line as the H2, eyebrow, no gradient). And retire the three copy families under 3.5% that carry a third of all shows
   ("Does Oregon know your name?" 3.3% and 2.0%, "You've compared five programs" 1%, "Could you make a list like this?" 1.5%)
   in favour of the shape of the 7 to 16% winners: the visitor's own mark, event, count and grade clock. Measure: per-variant click
   rate after 100 shows each.
7. **Accounts: the existing-email trap and the email confirmation on phones.** (checked) Signing up with an email that already
   has an account answers 200 with an empty `identities` array (Supabase anti-enumeration) and `account.js:283-286` sends the
   visitor to "Check your email"; no email ever comes. Fix (S): detect `r.user.identities.length === 0`, switch to sign-in with
   "That email already has an account." On phones the confirmation link opens in whichever browser the mail app uses, never the
   Instagram or TikTok webview the visit started in, and the sheet never notices. Fix (M): keep verification but as a 6-digit code
   typed in place (Supabase email template `{{ .Token }}`, `POST /auth/v1/verify` type signup), with the link as fallback; log
   `confirmed-*` and `signup-fail-*` events so the 92% can be split into "never confirmed" and "confirmed, abandoned". Also tell the
   whole price up front on the wall ("Free account in three short steps: email and password, confirm your email, add your events
   and grade").
8. **One mark format, and estimates marked.** (checked) The matches list prints the stored strings: 53.0 · 55 · 53.00 · 52.39 in one
   column; Long Jump rows mix "6.76m" with "22' 6" (6.85m)"; official charts print "x", "N/A", "4:10:00" and "**45.75 (150ft)" raw.
   1,996 of 9,346 conference levels are division medians (`est` flag: D3 36%, NAIA 42%, men's pole vault 40%), the footnote says
   they are "marked as estimates", and boot (`app.js:41`) drops the flag so no row is marked. Fix (M): one `fmtMark(event, value)`
   used by `cards()`, `disp()`, the official tables, the AI's tables and `schools.mjs` (times m:ss.ss or ss.ss, field `48' 0"
   (14.64m)`, converted values with a leading ≈); keep the flag at boot and show a muted "est" chip with a truthful legend.
9. **A cold phone load shows three pages before the real one.** (checked) Before the bundle runs a school page is a breadcrumb, a
   60 px headline, the "Quick answer" and an at-a-glance table; the app then replaces all of it (header at 2.7 s, band at 3.1 s on
   slow 4G) and throws the quick answer away; the home page shows a system-font link list, then an empty shell, then the search
   card. Every app page waits for all of `db.json` (234 KB served; about half is school-page detail) and a `raw.githubusercontent.com`
   index before the first render; a school page then fetches about 210 KB of site-wide JSON in 8 requests, three of them three
   times, to decorate one school. React, ReactDOM, react-router and react-helmet (54 KB brotli, 38% of the JS) and Tailwind's
   output (10 KB) ship to insert one static string. Fix, in order: (S) keep the quick answer as one line under the band; (M)
   prerender the app shell (header, band, skeleton rows) so boot is a content swap; (M) per-school facts JSON embedded in the
   page and `db-core.json` for the first paint; (M) render before the GitHub index arrives; (M) plain module entry instead of the
   React shell. Budgets: LCP under 2.0 s on slow 4G, CLS under 0.05 (desktop search URLs are at 0.23, the rankings tab at 0.30
   because the hero collapses and the rankings stylesheet attaches after the markup; decide the hero state from the URL before
   paint and bundle `rankings.css`).
10. **Results opens on the men's 100m D1 elites and needs a 3.9 MB file.** (checked) `getRank()` fetches the full-depth season file
    from GitHub with `cache:'no-cache'`, then conference meets (508 KB), then wind data, to show 10 rows; on slow 4G the first row
    appears after about 26 s. The default ignores the visitor's own search. Fix (M): build a head file per season (top 100 per
    event, about 60 to 80 KB), render from it, load depth in the background; open on the visitor's event and gender from
    `prIntent` (1600m → 1500m, 3200m → 5000m, 300mH → 400mH); accept `?g=&e=` deep links. Results users are the most engaged cohort
    (average 702 s), so this is worth the day.

including static ones; the focus ring on buttons and links is the 2 px violet outline; light-mode text colours all meet 4.5:1 by

## Not adopted, and why

- "Add recruiting questionnaire and coaching-staff links to every school page": conflicts with the rule shipped today (commit
  75b13adb, "show the what, sell the how": the site shows standards and results and hands outreach to the evaluation). Dropped.
- "Replace the free-limit wall with a softer prompt" variants: the 15-row wall and the finish-first rule are decisions; the plan
  changes what fills the 15 rows and the wall's copy, not the wall.
- "Use an LLM for the Recruiting AI's misses": rule (no API key, no cost). The basics intent table does the job for the top
  questions.
- The first-pass "Score colour 1/2" style findings are folded into the scorecard above rather than listed as separate items.
